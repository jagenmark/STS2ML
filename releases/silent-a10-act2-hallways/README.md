## What changed

The selected development variant is **general-pilot**. It reached the Act 2 boss 14 times and Act 3 6 times on the 192 locked development seeds. The previous model reached the boss 14 times and Act 3 four times. A linear fight selector also reached Act 3 six times, with 12 boss entries. The predeclared boss-entry tiebreak selected the formation table. Its saved Act 2 starts reproduced all 55 original terminal floors. The finalist's complete lobby rerun reached Act 3 6 times.

The policy selects its combat plan from the visible enemies when a regular or elite Act 2 fight opens. The training branches use exact native snapshots offline. Inference reads the normal public state. The model manifest includes every inherited draft, room, deck and combat checkpoint.

- `BOWLBUG_EGG+BOWLBUG_ROCK` uses `guard`.
- `BOWLBUG_NECTAR+BOWLBUG_ROCK` uses `pressure`.
- `EXOSKELETON+EXOSKELETON+EXOSKELETON` uses `guard`.
- `HUNTER_KILLER` uses `hunter_specialist`.
- `OVICOPTER` uses `beam`.
- `TUNNELER` uses `tempo`.

Hunter Killer applies Tender on its opening move. Each subsequent card costs one Strength and one Dexterity until that turn ends. The experimental combat scoring discounts the successive Shivs generated by a card. The optional turn planner deducts only future card plays from the current hand previews. The comparison also evaluated a card-level ranker and voluntary potion use. A native Swift Potion exposes its draw count in `vars.Cards`. Development variants with `draw_potions` read that field and estimate affordable defense from the public deck. The selected checkpoint records which options are active.

## Continue training

Extract `training-state.zip` to a fresh directory. It contains 1174 verified fight openings from 353 archived natural runs, a separate batch from the preceding model, the matched style outcomes, Hunter play-level alternatives, the original training receipts, and each fitted model's exact training and validation inventory. Native labels carry the host, teacher weights and source digests. The seed lock reserves development and test seeds separately.

```powershell
Expand-Archive "$archive/training-state.zip" artifacts/act2-training
$t = "artifacts/act2-training"
$base = "releases/silent-a10-encounter-draft/challenger/policy.json"
& $Python scripts/fit-act2-hallways.py --labels "$t/hunter-labels" --bundle $base --seed-lock "$archive/seed-lock.json" --margin 0.1 --output artifacts/hunter-refit
& $Python scripts/fit-act2-hallways.py --labels "$t/general-labels" --bundle $base --seed-lock "$archive/seed-lock.json" --hunter-model artifacts/hunter-refit/policy/policy.json --output artifacts/act2-new-fit
```

To reproduce an earlier table fit, add `--fit-inventory` with its matching `fits/<name>/fit.json` from the archive and use that fit's original settings and specialist. The same option is available for `scripts/fit-act2-plays.py`. The complete saved seed lists and candidate selection are in `seed-lock.json` and `selection.json`. The compressed per-seed outcomes and deduplicated action sequences support independent analysis and replay.
